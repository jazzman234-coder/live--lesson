1. Quick questions

In your Google Slides or Canva presentation, write these questions and answer them:

  1. What does physical fitness mean?
  2. Why is fitness important for everyday life?
  3. What types of fitness do you already know?
  4. What sports need different types of fitness?
Teacher support: These are introductory research questions. Do not replace them with a different retrieval task; they are part of the required student presentation.
  1. Physical fitness: being able to do physical activity and everyday tasks effectively without becoming tired too quickly.
  2. Importance: fitness can help people complete everyday activities, participate in sport and recreation, and support health and wellbeing.
  3. Possible types: cardiovascular endurance, muscular strength, muscular endurance, flexibility, agility, speed, coordination, balance, power and reaction time.
  4. Sports: different sports need different combinations. For example, soccer needs speed, agility, cardiovascular endurance and coordination.

Teacher fact bank — support only

This supports students who are stuck without changing the task requirements.

Cardiovascular Endurance HEALTH

Definition: ability of the heart and lungs to keep supplying oxygen during activity for a long time.

Sport: distance running.

Test: beep test.

Muscular Strength HEALTH

Definition: the amount of force a muscle or muscle group can produce.

Sport: weightlifting.

Test: handgrip dynamometer.

Muscular Endurance HEALTH

Definition: ability of muscles to keep working repeatedly over time.

Sport: rowing.

Test: timed wall sit or curl-up test.

Flexibility HEALTH

Definition: ability to move a joint through a wide range of movement.

Sport: gymnastics.

Test: sit-and-reach test.

Agility SKILL

Definition: ability to change direction quickly while staying in control.

Sport: basketball.

Test: Illinois agility test.

Speed SKILL

Definition: ability to move quickly.

Sport: sprinting or soccer.

Test: timed 20 m sprint.

Coordination SKILL

Definition: ability to use body parts together smoothly and accurately.

Sport: tennis.

Test: alternate-hand wall toss.

Balance SKILL

Definition: ability to stay stable while still or moving.

Sport: gymnastics.

Test: stork stand.

Power SKILL

Definition: ability to produce force quickly.

Sport: volleyball jump.

Test: standing broad jump.

Reaction Time SKILL

Definition: how quickly a person begins to respond to a signal or event.

Sport: goalkeeper reacting to a shot.

Test: ruler-drop test.

Teacher prompts for students who are stuck

ProblemPrompt
Definition copied or too technical“Can you explain that in words a Year 7 student would understand?”
Sport example missing“Which sport clearly uses this component?”
Test confused with training“Is that an activity used to measure the component, or an exercise used to improve it?”
Strength and endurance confused“Is this about maximum force, or keeping the muscles working for longer?”
Speed and agility confused“Is the athlete moving quickly in a straight line, or changing direction quickly?”
